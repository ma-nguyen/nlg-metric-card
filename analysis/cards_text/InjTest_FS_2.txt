## Participant metric card
## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

a. Did a perturbed output score higher than the gold output?
No, the perturbed outputs for both injections achieved lower scores than the gold summary, namely 0.125 and 0.099 vs. 0.286.

b. Were the scores similar for every injection?  
The scores were different, depending on the injection. The longer injection 1 received a slightly lower penalty than the shorter injection 2, -55.8% and -65.6% respectively. Thus, the metric is somewhat sensitive for the two perturbations, with the shorter injection being penalized stronger.

## 2. Suitability of bartscore-avg-f for summarization

### a. Strengths  
- Both injections achieved a moderate-to-high change, falling under 50-69% category, which might indicate that the metric effectively penalizes non-informative statements.

- The metric is on a positive scale (0-1), which can be easily interpreted.

### b. Weaknesses
- None of the injections showed a high or a very high change in the metric score, which may suggest that the metric still needs improvement for reliable detection of non-summary injections.

### c. Recommendation  
Overall, the metric is not recommended for scoring the quality of the summary. Even though the metric consistently detects every non-summary injection, the change rate is only moderate to high (50-69%).

### d. Needed Improvements  
1. Off-topic sensitivity: increase the penalty for non-summary statements so that injections could achieve a higher change-rate, corresponding to 70% and higher.
2. Human in the loop: It may be helpful to include finetuning of the metric according to human perception, so that the metric would be more aligned with human knowledge.
