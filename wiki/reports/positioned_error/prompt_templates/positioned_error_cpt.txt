The setup of the test:
The dataset used is WikiText-103.
The metric scores a set of 1000 gold open-ended texts each having a length around 256 tokens.
Then it scores a set of perturbed open-ended texts which got generated by
randomly shuffling 10 consecutive tokens in the beginning, middle or end of each
gold open-ended text (shuffling-test) or replacing 10 consecutive tokens in the beginning,
middle or end with 10 random tokens from the vocabulary in the used dataset (random-test).

A human evaluates as follows:
1. A perturbed open-ended text always scores lower than the gold open-ended text.
2. If the perturbed open-ended texts contains 10 consecutive tokens without semantic
meaning to human readers, the human readers would rate the perturbed texts with a minimal score,
it is possible that the metric has no minimal score.
