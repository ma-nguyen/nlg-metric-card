### 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  
**a.** No perturbed output scored higher than the gold output. All perturbations resulted in lower scores.  
**b.** Scores varied significantly by location. End perturbations caused the largest drops (‑98.1% for shuffling, ‑99.2% for random), while middle and beginning perturbations showed smaller changes (e.g., ‑13.4% for shuffling-middle).  
**c.** Scores differed between perturbation types. Random replacements caused larger drops in the middle (‑63.6% vs. ‑13.4% for shuffling), while shuffling had a larger impact at the beginning (‑42.4% vs. ‑32.7% for random).

### 2. Suitability of MAUVE-GPT2-base for Open-Ended Text Generation  
**a. Strengths**  
The metric exhibits **very high** sensitivity (90%-100% change rate) for:  
- End-located perturbations (shuffling: ‑98.1%, random: ‑99.2%).  
It shows **moderate-to-high** sensitivity (50%-69%) for:  
- Middle-located random replacements (‑63.6%).  

**b. Weaknesses**  
The metric shows **low-to-moderate** sensitivity (30%-49%) for:  
- Beginning-located perturbations (shuffling: ‑42.4%, random: ‑32.7%).  
It shows **low** sensitivity (10%-29%) for:  
- Middle-located shuffling (‑13.4%).  

**c. Recommendation**  
**No**. Only 3/6 perturbation variants (end-shuffling, end-random, middle-random) achieved ≥50% change rates, falling short of the >5 threshold.  

**d. Improvements Needed**  
Enhance sensitivity to:  
1. **Beginning-located perturbations** (both types), where change rates were suboptimal (low-to-moderate).  
2. **Middle-located shuffling**, which had the weakest response (low sensitivity).  
Mechanisms to better capture semantic disruptions in early/mid-text regions are critical for robust open-ended generation evaluation.
