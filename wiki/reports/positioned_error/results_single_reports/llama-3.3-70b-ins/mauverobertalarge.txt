### 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  
**a.** No perturbed output scored higher than the gold output. All perturbations resulted in lower scores.  
**b.** Scores varied significantly by location:  
- **Shuffling**: Largest drop at the end (-73.9%, high), smallest in the middle (-36.7%, low to moderate).  
- **Random**: Largest drops at beginning/end (very high: -96.8%/-96.0%), smaller in the middle (-87.6%, high).  
**c.** Scores differed substantially between perturbation types. Random-test consistently caused larger drops (very high/high: -87.6% to -96.8%) than shuffling (moderate/low: -36.7% to -73.9%).  

### 2. Suitability of MAUVE-RoBERTa-Large for Open-Ended Text Generation  
**a. Strengths**  
- Detected **random-test perturbations** with **very high sensitivity** (≥87.6% drop), especially at text boundaries (beginning/end: ≥96.0%).  
- Showed **strong sensitivity** to end-shuffling (-73.9%, high).  
- Consistently assigned lower scores to perturbations than gold texts.  

**b. Weaknesses**  
- **Inconsistent sensitivity to shuffling**: Middle-shuffling drop was **low to moderate** (-36.7%), failing to reflect severe semantic disruption.  
- **Location bias**: Sensitivity varied by position (e.g., middle perturbations were penalized less than boundaries).  

**c. Recommendation**  
**No**. Only 4/6 variants (random-test: all locations; shuffling: end) achieved **high or very high** change rates (≥70%). Middle-shuffling (-36.7%) and beginning-shuffling (-63.8%) fell below this threshold.  

**d. Improvements Needed**  
- Enhance sensitivity to **mid-text disruptions** (e.g., shuffling in middle).  
- Reduce **positional bias** to ensure uniform detection of perturbations across locations.  
- Investigate why shuffling (which destroys local coherence) is penalized less severely than random replacements.
