## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

**a. Did a perturbed output score higher than the gold output? If yes, which one.**  
No perturbed output scored higher than the gold output in any variation.  

**b. Were the scores similar for every location, meaning the scores of beginning, middle and end were similar?**  
No. The score changes varied significantly across locations:  
- **Shuffling-test**: End perturbations (-73.9%) had the largest drop, while middle perturbations (-36.7%) had the smallest.  
- **Random-test**: Beginning and end perturbations (-96.8% and -96.0%) had nearly identical drops, while middle perturbations (-87.6%) had a smaller but still substantial drop.  

**c. Were the scores similar for shuffling and random-test?**  
No. The **random-test** consistently showed higher score drops (very high to high) compared to the **shuffling-test** (moderate to high to low to moderate).  

---

## 2. Suitability of MAUVE-RoBERTa-Large for Open-Ended Text Generation  

### a. Strengths  
- **High sensitivity to random perturbations**: All random-test variations (beginning, middle, end) showed **high to very high** score drops (≥70% change).  
- **Strong detection of end perturbations**: Shuffling at the end (-73.9%) and random perturbations at the end (-96.0%) demonstrated **high to very high** sensitivity.  

### b. Weaknesses  
- **Low sensitivity to middle shuffling**: Middle shuffling (-36.7%) showed only **low to moderate** sensitivity, failing to align with human expectations of significant degradation.  
- **Inconsistent sensitivity to beginning shuffling**: Beginning shuffling (-63.8%) showed **moderate to high** sensitivity, but this was lower than the **very high** sensitivity observed in random perturbations.  

### c. Recommendation  
**No**. The metric achieved **high or higher sensitivity in 4 out of 6 variants** (shuffling-end, random-beginning, random-middle, random-end), falling short of the threshold (>5 variants).  

### d. What Needs Improvements  
- **Enhanced sensitivity to middle perturbations**: The metric underperforms in detecting semantic degradation when perturbations occur in the middle of texts.  
- **Enforcement of minimal scores**: While random-test perturbations achieved near-zero scores, the metric may not consistently enforce minimal scores for semantically nonsensical outputs (e.g., middle shuffling).  

---  
**Note**: While MAUVE-RoBERTa-Large demonstrates strong performance in many scenarios, it cannot fully substitute human evaluation, particularly for nuanced perturbations in central text regions.
