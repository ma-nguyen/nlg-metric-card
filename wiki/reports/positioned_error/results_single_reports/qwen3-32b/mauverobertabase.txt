## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

**a. Did a perturbed output score higher than the gold output? If yes, which one.**  
No perturbed output scored higher than the gold output in any variation.  

**b. Were the scores similar for every location, meaning the scores of beginning, middle and end were similar?**  
No. The scores for perturbations at the **end** showed significantly larger drops (46.9% and 47.0%) compared to the **beginning** (0.4%–7.1%) and **middle** (1.4%–5.3%).  

**c. Were the scores similar for shuffling and random-test?**  
No. While both perturbation types showed minimal changes for beginning and middle perturbations, the **end perturbations** had nearly identical score drops (46.9% vs. 47.0%) across shuffling and random-test.  

---

## 2. Suitability of MAUVE-RoBERTa-Base for Open-Ended Text Generation  

### a. Strengths  
- **High sensitivity to end perturbations**: Both shuffling and random-test at the end resulted in "low to moderate" score changes (46.9%–47.0%), aligning with human expectations for semantically disrupted text.  

### b. Weaknesses  
- **Low sensitivity to beginning/middle perturbations**: Score changes for beginning (0.4%–7.1%) and middle (1.4%–5.3%) perturbations were "very low" to "low," failing to reflect human perception of disrupted coherence.  
- **Inability to detect minimal human scores**: For end perturbations with 10 consecutive nonsensical tokens, the metric did not assign a minimal score (e.g., 0.51 vs. 0.96), indicating a gap in aligning with human judgment.  

### c. Recommendation  
**No**, the metric does not meet the threshold of >5 variants with high change rate or higher. Only 2 variants (end shuffling and end random) achieved "low to moderate" change rates.  

### d. What Needs Improvements  
- Enhance sensitivity to perturbations in the **beginning and middle** of texts.  
- Improve alignment with human evaluation for semantically nonsensical sequences (e.g., assigning minimal scores for end perturbations).  

**Note**: The best metric can still fail to fully substitute human evaluation. MAUVE-RoBERTa-Base requires refinement to better capture human-like judgments of coherence and semantic validity.
