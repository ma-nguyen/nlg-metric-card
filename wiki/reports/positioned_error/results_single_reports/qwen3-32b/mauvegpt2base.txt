## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

**a. Did a perturbed output score higher than the gold output? If yes, which one.**  
No perturbed output scored higher than the gold output in any variation.  

**b. Were the scores similar for every location, meaning the scores of beginning, middle and end were similar?**  
No. The score changes varied significantly across locations:  
- **Shuffling-test**: End perturbations caused a drastic drop (-98.1%), while middle perturbations had minimal impact (-13.4%).  
- **Random-test**: End perturbations caused the most severe drop (-99.2%), whereas middle perturbations had a moderate decline (-63.6%).  

**c. Were the scores similar for shuffling and random-test?**  
No. The **random-test** showed more severe score drops in the middle (-63.6% vs. -13.4% in shuffling-test) and end (-99.2% vs. -98.1% in shuffling-test). The **shuffling-test** had a larger drop in the beginning (-42.4% vs. -32.7% in random-test).  

---

## 2. Suitability of MAUVE-GPT2-base for Open-Ended Text Generation  

### a. Strengths  
- **High sensitivity to end perturbations**: Both shuffling (-98.1%) and random-test (-99.2%) at the end caused very high score drops, aligning with human expectations for semantically meaningless text.  
- **Moderate sensitivity to middle perturbations in random-test**: The -63.6% drop in the middle for random-test reflects human perception of disrupted coherence.  

### b. Weaknesses  
- **Low sensitivity to middle perturbations in shuffling-test**: Only a -13.4% drop, which is inconsistent with human expectations for semantically disrupted text.  
- **Moderate sensitivity to beginning perturbations**: Both shuffling (-42.4%) and random-test (-32.7%) showed low-to-moderate changes, suggesting limited detection of perturbations in the text’s opening.  

### c. Recommendation  
**No**, the metric does not meet the threshold for recommendation. Only **3 out of 6 variants** (end shuffling, end random, middle random) achieved high or very high change rates (≥70%).  

### d. What Needs Improvements  
- **Enhanced sensitivity to middle perturbations**: The metric underperforms in detecting disruptions in the middle of texts, where semantic coherence is critical.  
- **Consistent detection of beginning perturbations**: The current sensitivity to beginning perturbations (32.7–42.4%) is insufficient to reflect human judgment.  
- **Differentiation between shuffling and random perturbations**: The metric should better distinguish between perturbation types, as random-test perturbations (e.g., middle) caused more severe drops than shuffling.  

**Note**: While MAUVE-GPT2-base demonstrates strong performance in detecting end perturbations, it cannot fully substitute human evaluation due to inconsistencies in sensitivity across text regions and perturbation types.
