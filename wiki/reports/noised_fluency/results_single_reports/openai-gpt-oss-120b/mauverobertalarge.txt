*Explanation of attributes*  

- **Monotonous decrease**: the MAUVE score consistently falls as the variation level rises.  
- **Superlinear decrease**: the magnitude of the score drop grows with each successive increase in variation. Only verb lemmatization and article removal show this pattern.  
- **Change‑rate**: determined by the largest absolute percentage drop within the test. All tests except article removal exhibit drops ≥ ‑74 % (classified as **high**); article removal’s largest drop (‑54 %) falls into the **low** category per the given thresholds.  

---

## 2. Suitability of mauve‑roberta‑large for open‑ended text generation  

A metric is recommended only when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** display both a **high change‑rate** *and* a **superlinear decrease**.  

- Tests with high change‑rate: 9 out of 10.  
- Tests with superlinear decrease: only 2 (verb lemmatization, article removal).  

Since fewer than 7 tests satisfy **both** conditions, **mauve‑roberta‑large is *not* recommended** as a reliable substitute for human evaluation of open‑ended text generation.
