*Definitions used:*  
- **Monotonous decrease** – scores strictly decline as the variation level grows.  
- **Superlinear decrease** – the magnitude of score loss grows faster with each step (i.e., successive drops become larger).  
- **Change rate** – classified as *low* (max drop ≤ ‑25 %), *moderate* (max drop ≤ ‑60 %), otherwise *high* (max drop approaches ‑100 %).

## 2. Suitability of mauve‑gpt2 for open‑ended text generation  

A metric is recommended only when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** exhibit both a **high change rate** *and* a **superlinear decrease**.  

- Perturbations with high change rate: word removal, punctuation noise, sentence‑middle swap, preposition removal, stop‑word removal, random local swap, truncate, random token repetition (8 tests).  
- Among these, only **word removal**, **punctuation noise**, and **preposition removal** also show superlinear decrease (3 tests).  

Since fewer than 7 tests satisfy both conditions, **mauve‑gpt2 is *not* recommended** as a reliable metric for evaluating open‑ended text generation.
