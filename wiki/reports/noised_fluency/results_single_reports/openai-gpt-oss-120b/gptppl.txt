## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

For each perturbation we assess:

* **Monotonous decrease** – yes if the gpt‑ppl score consistently rises (i.e., quality consistently falls) as the variation grows.  
* **Superlinear decrease** – yes if the incremental change grows with each step (accelerating degradation).  
* **Change‑rate** – based on the largest reported change:  
  * **low**  ≤ 25 %  
  * **moderate** ≤ 60 %  
  * **high**  > 60 %

## 2. Suitability of **gpt‑ppl** for **open‑ended text generation**

A metric is deemed suitable when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** exhibit **both a high change‑rate and a superlinear decrease**.  

- Tests with **high + superlinear**:  
  * Word removal  
  * Preposition removal  
  * Stop‑word removal  
  * Word‑swap  

Only **4** out of the 10 perturbation families satisfy both conditions, which is far below the required majority.

**Recommendation:** **gpt‑ppl is *not* recommended** as a reliable automatic metric for evaluating open‑ended text generation, because it fails to show the strong, superlinear sensitivity to a majority of perturbations that human judgments display.
