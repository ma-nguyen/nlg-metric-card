## 2. Suitability of mauve-roberta for open-ended text generation

**Not recommended.**  
Only **4 out of 10 perturbation tests** (Word Removal, Stop-word Removal, Word Swap, Word Repetition) exhibit both **high change rate** and **superlinear decrease**, falling short of the threshold (>7 tests). While the metric shows sensitivity to severe perturbations (e.g., -99.5% for 50% word removal), its failure to consistently reflect superlinear human-like penalization for most perturbations limits its suitability for evaluating open-ended text generation.
