## 2. Suitability of gpt-ppl for open-ended text generation

**Not recommended.**  
Only 5 out of 10 perturbation tests exhibit both high change rate and superlinear decrease. The remaining 5 tests show either insufficient sensitivity (low/moderate change rate) or lack of superlinear response to perturbations. This fails to align with human evaluation expectations, which require disproportionately strong (superlinear) degradation in quality scores as perturbations increase.
