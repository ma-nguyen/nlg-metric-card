### a. Strengths  
- **"Answer:Yes,this is a really coherent and consistent summary. And yes,it is relevant."** (Change: -99.6%, *very high*): The metric strongly penalizes this injection, which superficially mimics a summary but lacks actual content.

### b. Weaknesses  
No injection types in this test demonstrated low or moderate change rates. However, the metric may struggle with more nuanced perturbations (e.g., summaries with partial relevance or paraphrased content not tested here).  

### c. Recommendation  
**Yes.** BERTScore-F successfully detects both tested non-summary injections with *very high* change rates, aligning with human evaluation criteria.  

### d. Additional Improvements  
While BERTScore-F performs well on these injection types, further testing is needed to evaluate its robustness against:  
1. **Contextually relevant but factually incorrect summaries.**  
2. **Summaries with partial overlap in key terms but poor coherence.**  
3. **Injections that mimic summary structure but lack substantive content (e.g., "The article discusses...").**  

The best metric can still fail to fully substitute human evaluation, particularly in edge cases requiring deeper contextual understanding.
