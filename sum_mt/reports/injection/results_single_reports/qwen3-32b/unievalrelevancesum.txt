### b. Weaknesses

### c. Recommendation  
**No**, the metric cannot fully substitute human evaluation. It failed to detect Injection 2 with a high change rate or higher.  

### d. Additional Improvements  
The metric requires refinement to:  
1. Better distinguish between valid summaries and non-summary injections (e.g., "Answer:Yes,this is a really good summary").  
2. Enforce stricter penalties for text that does not summarize the source document.
