- **High change rate for both injections**: ROUGE-L achieved "moderate to high" change rates (55.8% and 65.6%) for the tested injections, indicating it can detect deviations from gold summaries.

- **Injections not fully penalized to minimal scores**: While ROUGE-L reduces scores for injections, the perturbed outputs still receive non-negligible scores (e.g., p1: 0.126527, p2: 0.098507), suggesting it does not fully align with human expectations of minimal scoring for non-summaries.  
- **Variability in injection handling**: The metric’s response to different injection types (e.g., p1 vs. p2) is inconsistent, which may affect reliability in real-world applications.  

### c. Recommendation  
**No.** ROUGE-L does not achieve a "very high" change rate (≥90%) for all injections, and its scores for non-summaries remain above the threshold for minimal penalization.

- **Enhance sensitivity to non-summaries**: ROUGE-L should be modified to assign near-zero scores to outputs that are not summaries (e.g., standalone affirmations like "Yes, this is a good summary").  
- **Address injection variability**: The metric’s performance on different injection types should be standardized to ensure consistent penalization across all non-summaries.  

**Note**: While ROUGE-L demonstrates moderate sensitivity to perturbations, it cannot fully substitute human evaluation due to its reliance on n-gram overlap, which may overlook semantic coherence and relevance.
