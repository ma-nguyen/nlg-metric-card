- The metric does not differentiate between qualitatively different non‑summaries (both injections receive almost identical low scores), which may limit diagnostic granularity.

### d. Needed Improvements  
1. **Define a non‑negative lower bound** (e.g., clamp scores at 0) to avoid negative outputs that can be confusing for users.  
2. **Calibrate the score range** so that the magnitude of change aligns more closely with human judgment scales (e.g., map the extreme drop to a standardized “minimal” quality score).  
3. **Increase diagnostic resolution** by allowing the metric to distinguish between different types of perturbations (e.g., irrelevant text vs. overly generic statements).
