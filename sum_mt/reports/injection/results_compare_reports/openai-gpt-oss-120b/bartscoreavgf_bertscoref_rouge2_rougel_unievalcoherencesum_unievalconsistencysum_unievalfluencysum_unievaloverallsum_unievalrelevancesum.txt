## Recommendation  

**Recommended metrics (high change rates):**  

- **bert_score_f** – exhibits very high negative changes (‑99.6 % and ‑107.0 %) for both injection types, clearly penalising non‑summarising text.  
- **rouge‑2** – also shows very high negative changes (‑90.9 % and ‑91.6 %), indicating strong sensitivity to the injected, irrelevant sentences.  

These two metrics consistently produce large drops in score when the input is a perturbed (non‑summary) injection, satisfying the human‑evaluation principle that perturbed summaries should receive substantially lower scores than gold summaries.

**Additional useful metric:**  

- **bartscore‑avg‑f** – moderate declines (‑36 % and ‑55 %) suggest it can differentiate between gold and injected texts, though not as sharply as the two recommended metrics.

**Metrics not recommended:**  

All **UniEval** variants (coherence, consistency, fluency, overall, relevance) display very low or even positive changes (ranging from +0.7 % to +15.3 %). They frequently assign higher scores to the injected sentences than to the gold summaries, failing to penalise irrelevant or nonsensical content. Consequently, they do not meet the human‑evaluation requirement of a minimal score for perturbed summaries and should not be relied upon as primary automatic evaluators for summarisation quality.
