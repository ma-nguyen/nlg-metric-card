## Recommendation  

**Recommended metrics with high change rates (≥50%):**  
1. **BERTScore-f** (ΔInj-1: -99.6%, ΔInj-2: -107.0%)  
2. **ROUGE-2** (ΔInj-1: -90.9%, ΔInj-2: -91.6%)  
3. **ROUGE-L** (ΔInj-2: -65.6%)  
4. **BARTScore-avg-f** (ΔInj-2: -54.9%)  

**Metrics to avoid:**  
- **UniEval-fluency(sum)** and **UniEval-relevance(sum)**: Both assign **higher scores to injections** (ΔInj-1: +4.4% and +15.3%, respectively), violating the human evaluation criterion that perturbed summaries should score lower.  
- **UniEval-overall(sum)**: While ΔInj-2 is moderate (-3.0%), ΔInj-1 is +4.7%, indicating inconsistent performance.  

**Key observations:**  
- **BERTScore-f** and **ROUGE-2** demonstrate the most robust discrimination between gold summaries and injections, with **>90% score drops** for perturbed inputs.  
- **UniEval metrics** (e.g., coherence, fluency) often fail to penalize injections effectively, particularly for injection_1, which is a non-summary text. This suggests poor alignment with human judgment criteria.  

**Note:** While BERTScore-f and ROUGE-2 show strong performance, they may still fail to fully capture human-like evaluation (e.g., coherence, relevance) in complex cases.
