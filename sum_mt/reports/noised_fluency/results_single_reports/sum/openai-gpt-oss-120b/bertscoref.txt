*Definitions applied*  

* **Monotonous decrease** – the score never rises when the variation level increases.  
* **Superlinear decrease** – the magnitude of the score drop grows with each increase in variation (i.e., incremental drops become larger).  
* **Change‑rate** – based on the largest absolute percentage change observed for the perturbation:  
  * low = ≤ 25 % drop,  
  * moderate = ≤ 60 % drop,  
  * high = > 60 % drop (or approaching the metric’s minimum).

## 2. Suitability of **bert_score_f** for **summarization**

A metric is deemed suitable when **most > 7** perturbation tests exhibit a **high change‑rate** *and* a **superlinear decrease**.  

- Only **one** perturbation (punctuation noise) shows superlinear behaviour, and none reach a high change‑rate.  
- The majority of tests either have low or moderate change‑rates and lack superlinear drops.

**Conclusion:** *bert_score_f* is **not recommended** as a reliable automatic metric for summarization, because it does not react strongly enough—or in a superlinear fashion—to the severity of the perturbations that humans penalise heavily.
