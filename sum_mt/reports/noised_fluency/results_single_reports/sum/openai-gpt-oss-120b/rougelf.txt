## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

For each perturbation type we assess three attributes:

* **Monotonous decrease** – the score never rises when the variation level increases.  
* **Super‑linear decrease** – the absolute drop between successive variation levels grows (i.e., the penalty accelerates).  
* **Change rate** – based on the largest absolute score change observed (‑25 % → *low*, ≤ ‑60 % → *moderate*, otherwise *high*).

| Perturbation | Monotonous decrease | Super‑linear decrease | Change rate |
|--------------|---------------------|-----------------------|-------------|
| lemmatize (verb randomisation) | yes | no | low |
| randomworddrop (word removal) | yes | no | low |
| noisepunct (punctuation noise) | yes | no | low |
| sentencemiddleswap (sentence‑middle swap) | yes | no | low |
| removepreposition (preposition removal) | yes | **yes** | low |
| removestopwords (stop‑word removal) | yes | no | low |
| randomlocalswap (local word swap) | yes | no | low |
| truncate (suffix removal) | **no** (scores increase) | no | low |
| removearticle (article removal) | yes | **yes** | low |
| randomtokenrep (token repetition) | yes | no | low |

**Explanation of the flags**

* All perturbations except *truncate* show a non‑increasing score as the variation grows, therefore “monotonous decrease = yes”.  
* Only the preposition‑removal and article‑removal tests exhibit an accelerating penalty (the absolute drop from the first to the second variation is larger than the first drop), thus “super‑linear decrease = yes” for those two.  
* The greatest absolute score change in any test never exceeds ‑20 % (the largest observed is –20 % for 50 % token repetition). Consequently every test falls into the **low** change‑rate category (‑25 % threshold not crossed).

## 2. Suitability of rougeL‑f for Summarisation  

A metric is deemed **reasonable** for the task when the majority ( > 7 out of the 10 perturbation tests ) display a **high** change rate **and** a **super‑linear** decrease.  

* rougeL‑f shows a **low** change rate for **all** perturbation types.  
* Only **two** tests (preposition removal, article removal) exhibit super‑linear behaviour, far fewer than the required seven.  

**Conclusion:**  
rougeL‑f is **not recommended** as a reliable automatic metric for summarisation, because it reacts only weakly to increasingly severe perturbations and fails to capture the super‑linear penalty that human judges apply to major fluency degradations.
