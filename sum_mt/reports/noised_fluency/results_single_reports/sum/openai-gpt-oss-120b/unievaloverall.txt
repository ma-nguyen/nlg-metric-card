## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

For each perturbation type we assess:

* **Monotonous decrease** – does the score consistently drop as the variation grows?  
* **Superlinear decrease** – does the magnitude of the drop increase with higher variation (i.e., each step causes a larger loss than the previous one)?  
* **Change rate** – based on the largest absolute change observed:  
  * **low**  if ≤ 25 % drop,  
  * **moderate** if ≤ 60 % drop,  
  * **high**  otherwise.

| Perturbation Type | Monotonous decrease | Superlinear decrease | Change rate |
|-------------------|---------------------|----------------------|-------------|
| verb lemmatization | **yes** | **no** | low |
| word removal | **yes** | **no** | high |
| punctuation noise | **yes** | **no** | low |
| sentence‑middle swap | **yes** | **no** | high |
| preposition removal | **yes** | **no** | moderate |
| stop‑word removal | **yes** | **no** | moderate |
| word swap (local) | **yes** | **no** | high |
| truncate (end‑cut) | **no** (score rises at 30 %) | **no** | moderate |
| article removal | **yes** | **no** | low |
| word repetition | **yes** | **no** | high |

*Explanation of the flags*  

* **Monotonous decrease** – all listed perturbations (except truncate) show a non‑increasing sequence of scores as the variation level grows.  
* **Superlinear decrease** – none of the perturbations exhibit an accelerating loss; the incremental drops are equal or smaller at higher variation levels.  
* **Change rate** – the highest observed drop per test determines the category (e.g., word removal reaches –85.5 %, therefore “high”).

## 2. Suitability of **unieval_overall** for **summarization**

A metric is deemed suitable when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** display **high change rates** **and** **superlinear decrease**.  

*Number of tests with high change rate*: 5 (word removal, sentence‑middle swap, word swap, word repetition, and verb lemmatization is low).  
*Number of tests with superlinear decrease*: 0.

Since the required condition (majority of tests with both high change rate **and** superlinear behavior) is not met, **unieval_overall is **not** recommended as a reliable automatic metric for summarization.**
