## 2. Suitability of unieval_coherence for Summarization  

The metric would be recommended only if the majority of perturbation tests ( > 7 ) exhibit a **high** change rate **and** a **superlinear** decrease.  

- High change‑rate tests: Random Word Drop, Sentence‑Middle‑Swap, Random Local Swap, Word Repetition (4 tests).  
- Superlinear decrease is observed only for Punctuation Noise and Article Removal (2 tests).  

Since fewer than seven perturbation types show both high sensitivity and superlinear behavior, **unieval_coherence is not recommended as a reliable automatic metric for summarization**.
