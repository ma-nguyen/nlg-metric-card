**Explanation of flags**

* **Monotonous decrease** – the rouge2‑f score never rises when the variation level increases.  
* **Superlinear decrease** – the magnitude of the score drop grows with each step (the incremental drop is larger than the previous one). None of the perturbations show this pattern.  
* **Change‑rate** – determined from the largest absolute score change in the test:  
  * *low* ≤ 25 % drop (e.g., punctuation, sentence‑swap, preposition, article).  
  * *moderate* ≤ 60 % drop (no test reaches this range).  
  * *high* > 25 % but < 60 % drop (word removal, stop‑word removal, random swap, token repetition).

## 2. Suitability of **rouge2‑f** for **summarization**

A metric is recommended only when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** exhibit a **high change‑rate** *and* a **superlinear decrease**.  

- High change‑rate tests: word removal, stop‑word removal, random word swap, token repetition (4 tests).  
- No test shows a superlinear decrease.  

Since the required condition (≥ 7 high‑change, superlinear tests) is not met, **rouge2‑f is *not* recommended as a reliable automatic metric for summarization**. It is relatively insensitive to many linguistic perturbations (especially punctuation and sentence‑order changes) and its score declines linearly rather than reflecting the steep penalisation that humans apply to severe fluency errors.
