## 2. Suitability of **unieval_consistency** for **summarization**

A metric is deemed suitable when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** display **high change rate** **and** **superlinear decrease**.  

* Only **one** perturbation (Word Removal) reaches a high change rate, and **none** show superlinear behavior.  
* The majority of tests are low or moderate in change rate, and all are non‑superlinear.

**Conclusion:** **unieval_consistency is *not* recommended** as a reliable automatic metric for summarization quality assessment. It reacts to many perturbations, but the magnitude of its response is generally modest and lacks the super‑linear sensitivity that aligns with human judgments of severe fluency degradations.
