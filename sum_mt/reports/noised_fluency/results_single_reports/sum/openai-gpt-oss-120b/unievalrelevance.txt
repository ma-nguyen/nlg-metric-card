*Explanation of attributes*  

- **Monotonous decrease**: the metric’s score never increases as the perturbation level grows.  
- **Superlinear decrease**: the absolute drop between successive perturbation levels grows monotonically; none of the tested perturbations exhibit this behaviour.  
- **Change‑Rate**: determined by the largest absolute change relative to the gold score (low ≤ 25 %, moderate ≤ 60 %, high > 60 %).  

---

## 2. Suitability of **unieval_relevance** for **summarization**

A metric is deemed suitable when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** show a **high change‑rate** **and** a **superlinear decrease**.  

- High change‑rate is observed for 6 perturbations (Word Removal, Sentence‑Middle‑Swap, Word‑Swap, Word Repetition, and two others).  
- **No** perturbation exhibits a superlinear decrease.  

Because the required combination (high change‑rate **and** superlinear) is not met for the majority of tests, **unieval_relevance is *not* recommended as a reliable automatic metric for summarization**.
