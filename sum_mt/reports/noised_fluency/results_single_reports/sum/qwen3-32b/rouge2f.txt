## 2. Suitability of ROUGE2-F for Summarization  
ROUGE2-F is **not recommended** for summarization evaluation. Only **2 out of 10 perturbation tests** (Word Removal and Word Swap) exhibit **high change rates** and **superlinear decrease**, while the remaining 8 tests show **low or moderate sensitivity** to perturbations. This fails to align with human evaluation expectations, where perturbations should induce **disproportionately strong score drops** (superlinear decrease) for severe fluency errors. The metric’s limited sensitivity to most perturbations suggests it inadequately reflects human judgment of summary quality.
