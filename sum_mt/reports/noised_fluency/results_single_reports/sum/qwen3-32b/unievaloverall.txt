## 2. Suitability of unieval_overall for summarization

**Recommendation: Not recommended**  
Only 3 out of 10 perturbation tests (Word Removal, Word Swap, Word Repetition) exhibit both high change rates and superlinear decreases. The remaining 7 tests show low or moderate sensitivity, and none achieve superlinear decreases. This fails to align with human evaluation patterns, which penalize severe fluency errors disproportionately. The metric lacks sufficient sensitivity to critical perturbations in summarization tasks.
