## 2. Suitability of bert_score_f for summarization

**Not recommended.**  
Only 3 out of 10 perturbation tests (Word Removal, Word Swap, Word Repetition) exhibit **high change rate** and **superlinear decrease**, which aligns with human evaluation expectations. The remaining tests show **moderate** or **low** sensitivity, and 3 tests (Truncate, Verb Lemmatization, Article Removal) fail to meet the criteria for superlinear decrease. This suggests bert_score_f is insufficiently sensitive to most perturbations for robust summarization evaluation.
