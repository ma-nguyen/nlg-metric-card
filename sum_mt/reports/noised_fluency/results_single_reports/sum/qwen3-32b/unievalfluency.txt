## 2. Suitability of unieval_fluency for summarization

**Not recommended.**  
While unieval_fluency demonstrates high sensitivity in 4 out of 10 perturbation tests (word removal, sentence middle swap, word swapping, word repetition) with both high change rates and superlinear decreases, it fails to meet the threshold of >7 tests with these properties. Additionally, 3 tests (punctuation noise, preposition removal, article removal) show only moderate or low sensitivity, and one test (truncation) lacks monotonous decrease entirely. The metric does not consistently align with human evaluation patterns, particularly in reflecting disproportionately strong penalties for severe fluency errors.
