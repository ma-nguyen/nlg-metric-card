## 2. Suitability of unieval_consistency for summarization

The unieval_consistency metric is **not recommended** for summarization evaluation. Only 2 out of 10 perturbation tests (Word Removal and Word Swap) demonstrate a high change rate, and 4 tests (Word Removal, Stop-word Removal, Word Swap, and Word Repetition) show superlinear decrease. The majority of perturbation tests exhibit low or moderate sensitivity, failing to meet the criteria of >7 tests with high change rate and superlinear decrease.
