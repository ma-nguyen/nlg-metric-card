## 2. Suitability of bert_score_f for machine translation

**Not recommended.**  
Only **1 perturbation test** (Word Removal) achieves a **high change rate** (>-60%), and **5 perturbation tests** (Word Removal, Stop-Word Removal, Word Swapping, Truncation, Word Repetition) exhibit **superlinear decrease**. This falls short of the threshold (>7 tests) required for strong alignment with human evaluation criteria, which emphasizes disproportionately strong penalties for severe perturbations.
