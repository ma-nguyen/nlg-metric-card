**Recommendation: Not recommended.**

**Rationale:**
- Only **2 out of 10 perturbation tests** (Remove Words and Truncate End Words) exhibit both high change rates and superlinear decreases.
- The majority of perturbation tests (8/10) show **linear decreases** or **moderate/high change rates without superlinearity**, which fails to align with human evaluation patterns (superlinear penalization of severe errors).
