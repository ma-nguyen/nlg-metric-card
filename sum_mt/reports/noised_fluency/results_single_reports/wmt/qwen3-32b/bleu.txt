## 2. Suitability of BLEU for Machine Translation

The BLEU metric is **not recommended** for the evaluation of machine translation based on the test results. While 5 out of 10 perturbation tests (Word Removal, Stop-word Removal, Word Swap, Truncation, and Word Repetition) show a superlinear decrease and moderate/high change rates, this does not meet the threshold of >7 tests required for recommendation. Additionally, several critical perturbations (e.g., Verb Lemmatization, Punctuation Noise) exhibit only low sensitivity, failing to align with human evaluation patterns that penalize severe fluency errors disproportionately. The metric's linear response to some perturbations and insufficient sensitivity to others indicates it does not fully capture human judgment of translation quality.
