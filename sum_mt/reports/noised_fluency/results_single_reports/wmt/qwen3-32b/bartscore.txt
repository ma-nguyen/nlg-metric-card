## 2. Suitability of BARTScore for Machine Translation  

**Recommendation:** **Not Recommended**  

**Rationale:**  
- Only **2 out of 10 perturbation tests** ("Word Removal" and "Truncate End") exhibit **superlinear decreases**, which aligns with human evaluation's disproportionate penalty for severe errors.  
- **High change rates** are observed in only **1 test** ("Word Removal"), while most tests show **low or moderate sensitivity**.  
- BARTScore fails to consistently reflect the **superlinear human penalty** for perturbations, particularly for critical fluency errors (e.g., word removal, truncation).  

**Conclusion:** BARTScore demonstrates limited sensitivity to perturbations in machine translation, particularly in capturing the severity of fluency degradation. It is not a robust substitute for human evaluation in this context.
