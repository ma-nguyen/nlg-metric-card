| Perturbation Type | Monotonous decrease | Superlinear decrease | Change rate |

| Random local swap | **yes** | **no** | low |

| Article removal | **yes** | **yes** | low |
| Random token repetition | **yes** | **no** | low |

*Definitions applied:*  

* **Monotonous decrease** – the score never rises as the variation level grows.  
* **Superlinear decrease** – the absolute drop between successive variation levels grows (i.e., later drops are larger than earlier ones).  
* **Change rate** – determined by the largest absolute percentage drop observed for the test:  
  * low ≤ 25 % drop,  
  * moderate ≤ 60 % drop,  
  * high > 60 % drop.  

---

## 2. Suitability of **bert_score_f** for **machine translation**

A metric is deemed suitable when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** exhibit **both a high change rate and a super‑linear decrease**.  

- Only the *Random word drop* test reaches a high change rate, but it does **not** show a super‑linear pattern.  
- All other tests either have low/moderate change rates or lack super‑linear behaviour (or both).  

**Result:** **bert_score_f is *not* recommended** as a primary evaluation metric for machine translation, because it fails to satisfy the required combination of high sensitivity and super‑linear penalisation across the majority of perturbation types.
