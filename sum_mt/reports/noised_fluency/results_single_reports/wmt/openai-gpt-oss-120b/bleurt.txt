*Definitions applied:*  
- **Monotonous decrease** – the score never rises as the variation level grows.  
- **Superlinear decrease** – the magnitude of the score drop grows with each step (accelerating loss).  
- **Change rate** – classified by the largest absolute percentage change:  
  - *low* ≤ –25 %  
  - *moderate* ≤ –60 %  
  - *high* > –60 % (or approaching the metric’s minimum).

---

## 2. Suitability of **bleurt** for **machine translation**

A metric is recommended only when **most (> 7)** perturbation tests exhibit **high change rate** *and* **superlinear decrease**.  

- **High change rate** is observed in 7 perturbation families (Word Removal, Sentence‑middle Swap, Preposition Removal, Stop‑word Removal, Random Local Swap, Truncate, Token Repetition).  
- **Superlinear decrease** is present in **only one** family (Word Removal).  

Since the required combination (high change rate **and** superlinear decrease in the majority of tests) is not met, **bleurt is *not* recommended** as a reliable automatic metric for machine‑translation quality assessment.
