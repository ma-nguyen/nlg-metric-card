## 1. Sensitivity to the Perturbation  

| Perturbation | Monotonous decrease | Super‑linear decrease | Change‑rate |
|--------------|---------------------|-----------------------|-------------|
| Verb lemmatization | **yes** | **no** | **low** |
| Word removal | **yes** | **yes** | **high** |
| Punctuation noise | **yes** | **no** | **low** |
| Sentence‑middle swap | **yes** | **no** | **low** |
| Preposition removal | **yes** | **no** | **low** |
| Stop‑word removal | **yes** | **no** | **moderate** |
| Random local swap | **yes** | **no** | **moderate** |
| Truncate (end‑word removal) | **yes** | **yes** | **moderate** |
| Article removal | **yes** | **no** | **low** |
| Token repetition | **yes** | **no** | **moderate** |

**Explanation of the flags**

* **Monotonous decrease** – In every test the bart_score becomes more negative as the perturbation intensity grows, therefore the flag is *yes* for all perturbations.  
* **Super‑linear decrease** – A super‑linear pattern is observed when the magnitude of the score drop accelerates with higher variation. This is evident for **Word removal** (the drop grows from –11.8 % up to –108.1 %) and for **Truncate**, where the last step (‑50.4 %) is markedly larger than the previous increments. All other perturbations show roughly linear or sub‑linear behaviour, so the flag is *no*.  
* **Change‑rate** – Determined from the largest absolute percentage drop within each perturbation set:  
  * *low* ≤ 25 % (e.g., verb lemmatization, punctuation noise, sentence‑middle swap, preposition removal, article removal).  
  * *moderate* > 25 % and ≤ 60 % (stop‑word removal, random local swap, truncate, token repetition).  
  * *high* > 60 % (word removal).

---

## 2. Suitability of **bart_score** for **machine translation**

A metric is recommended only when **most > 7** perturbation tests exhibit **high change‑rate** **and** **super‑linear decrease**.  

- Number of perturbation tests with **high** change‑rate: **1** (Word removal).  
- Number of tests with **super‑linear** behaviour: **2** (Word removal, Truncate).  

Since the required condition (≥ 8 tests with both properties) is far from satisfied, **bart_score is *not* recommended** as a reliable automatic metric for machine‑translation quality assessment.
