## 2. Suitability of bert_score_f for machine translation

**Recommendation**: **Not recommended**  

**Rationale**:  
- Only **2/10 perturbation tests** (Word Removal, Word Swap) exhibit **high change rates** and **superlinear decreases**, which align with human evaluation expectations.  
- **5/10 tests** (Verb Lemmatization, Punctuation Noise, Sentence Middle Swap, Preposition Removal, Article Removal) show **low sensitivity** (change rate: low).  
- **3/10 tests** (Stop-word Removal, Word Swap, Word Repetition) show **moderate sensitivity** but lack **superlinear decreases** in 2/3 cases.  
- The metric fails to consistently reflect the **disproportionately strong human penalty** for severe fluency errors (superlinear decrease) across most perturbation types.  

**Conclusion**: While bert_score_f shows some sensitivity to perturbations, it does not robustly align with human evaluation patterns for machine translation.
