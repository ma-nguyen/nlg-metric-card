## 2. Suitability of BLEU for Machine Translation  

**Recommendation**: **Not recommended**.  

**Rationale**:  
- Only **2/10 perturbations** (Word Removal, Truncate End Words) show **superlinear decreases**, which aligns with human evaluation's disproportionate penalty for severe errors.  
- **6/10 perturbations** (e.g., Punctuation Noise, Stop-Word Removal) exhibit **moderate change rates**, but lack superlinearity.  
- **2/10 perturbations** (Verb Lemmatization, Sentence Middle Swap) show **low sensitivity**, failing to reflect human perception of fluency degradation.  
- While BLEU detects degradation in most cases, its **limited superlinearity** and **inconsistent sensitivity** to critical fluency errors (e.g., word removal, truncation) make it **insufficient as a standalone metric** for machine translation evaluation.  

**Conclusion**: BLEU partially aligns with human evaluation but fails to meet the criteria for robustness and sensitivity required for reliable machine translation assessment.
