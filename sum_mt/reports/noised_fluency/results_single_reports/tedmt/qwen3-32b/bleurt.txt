## 2. Suitability of BLEURT for Machine Translation

**Recommendation**: Not recommended.  

**Analysis**:  
- **High change rate** and **superlinear decrease** are observed in **6/10** perturbation tests (Word Removal, Preposition Removal, Stop-word Removal, Word Swapping, Truncate, Word Repetition).  
- However, **4/10** tests (Verb Lemmatization, Punctuation Noise, Sentence Middle Swap, Article Removal) show **moderate or low sensitivity** and **no superlinear decrease**.  
- Since the threshold for recommendation requires **>7 tests** to meet both criteria, BLEURT fails to align with human evaluation patterns in a sufficient number of cases.  

**Conclusion**: BLEURT demonstrates strong sensitivity in some perturbation types but lacks consistent superlinear responsiveness across all critical fluency and accuracy dimensions in machine translation.
