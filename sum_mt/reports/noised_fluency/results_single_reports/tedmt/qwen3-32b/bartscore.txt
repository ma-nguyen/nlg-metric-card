## 2. Suitability of BARTScore for Machine Translation

**Recommendation**: **Not recommended**.  

**Analysis**:  
- **High change rate** (≥ -60%): 1 test (Word Removal).  
- **Superlinear decrease**: 5 tests (Word Removal, Stop-word Removal, Word Swap, Truncation, Word Repetition).  
- **Threshold for recommendation**: >7 tests with high change rate and superlinear decrease.  
- **Result**: Only 1 test meets the high change rate threshold, and 5 tests meet the superlinear decrease threshold. The metric fails to align with human evaluation expectations, as it does not consistently reflect the disproportionately strong penalties for severe fluency errors.
