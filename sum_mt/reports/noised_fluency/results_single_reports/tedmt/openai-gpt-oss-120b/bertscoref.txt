*Notes*  
- “Monotonous decrease” is **yes** when every higher variation yields a lower score than the preceding one.  
- “Superlinear decrease” is **yes** only for the truncate test, where the magnitude of score loss grows increasingly with larger truncation.  
- “Change rate” is classified according to the largest absolute score drop observed for the test:  
  - **low** ≤ 25 % (e.g., verb lemmatization, punctuation noise)  
  - **moderate** > 25 % and ≤ 60 % (e.g., stop‑word removal, truncate)  
  - **high** > 60 % (random word removal).

---

## 2. Suitability of **bert_score_f** for **Machine Translation**

A metric is deemed suitable when **most (> 7) perturbation tests** exhibit a **high change rate** *and* a **superlinear decrease**.  

- Only **one** test (random word removal) shows a high change rate.  
- Only **one** test (truncate) shows a superlinear decrease.  

Therefore, **bert_score_f is *not* recommended** as a reliable automatic metric for machine‑translation quality assessment.
