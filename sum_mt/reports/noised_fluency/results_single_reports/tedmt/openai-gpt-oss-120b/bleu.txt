**Explanation of attributes**

* **Monotonous decrease** – All listed perturbations show a non‑increasing BLEU score as the variation level grows.
* **Super‑linear decrease** – Detected when the magnitude of score loss grows with each successive variation step (e.g., Noise punctuation, Truncate, Remove articles).  
* **Change‑rate** – Determined from the largest absolute percentage drop within each perturbation set:  
  * *low* (≤ 25 % drop), *moderate* (≤ 60 % drop), *high* (> 60 % drop).

## 2. Suitability of **bleu** for **machine translation**

To be considered a reasonable metric, a majority (> 7) of perturbation tests must exhibit **high** change‑rate **and** super‑linear decrease.  

Only the *Random word drop* test shows a high change‑rate, and it does **not** display super‑linear behaviour. The remaining tests either have low/moderate change‑rates or lack super‑linear decline. Consequently, **bleu** does **not** meet the recommended criteria for machine‑translation evaluation under the presented perturbation framework.  

**Recommendation:** **Not recommended**.
